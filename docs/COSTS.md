# Costs

Figures are from the vendors' public pricing pages, read on 22 November 2026, in US dollars. Prices change. Check each page before you decide. Nothing here is a quote.

## A realistic month for one small shop

| Item | Basis | Per month |
|---|---|---|
| Vercel Pro | $20 per month. The Hobby plan has usage caps, and its terms are aimed at personal use, so use Pro for a business. | $20 |
| Neon Postgres | Launch plan is pay as you go: $0.106 per compute hour, $0.35 per GB month. A tiny database that scales to zero is a few dollars. Always on at the smallest size is about $19. | $3 to $20 |
| Texts (Canada) | $0.0083 per segment on Twilio plus a carrier fee of about $0.0064 to $0.0087 per segment. About 600 texts a month (reservation, ready, expiry notices) is roughly | $9 to $15 |
| Email for the accountant | Resend free is 3,000 emails a month. One a month is well inside it. | $0 |
| Market rate feed (optional) | Depends on the provider. It only suggests a morning rate. Not priced here. | ask before choosing |
| Domain | About $15 to $25 a year | about $2 |

Rough total: **$35 to $60 per month**, before the optional rate feed. Free tiers are fine for the pitch. A real shop should not run on them.

Neon's free plan includes 100 compute hours a month per project and suspends compute when it runs out. That is fine for the demo, and not for a live shop.

## Shopify, honestly

Shopify would cover checkout, payments, shipping and a mature admin out of the box, and it has plans from a modest monthly price (see shopify.com/pricing). What it does not do natively is price every piece from a daily gold rate, hold a rate for a reservation, buy old gold with an ID check and a metal ledger, run layaway, or send an HST summary to the accountant. Those need apps or custom code and you would be bending a general shop to fit. If the shop wants to sell jewellery online with delivery, Shopify is a strong choice for that part. If the aim is a rate driven showroom with reserve for pickup and books, this build fits better. They can also live side by side.
