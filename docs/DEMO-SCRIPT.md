# Five minute demo

Use the pitch link on a phone and a laptop. In demo mode both see the same data. If you are running mock mode locally, use two tabs of one browser instead.

Before you start, open the admin on the laptop, sign in as the owner and press Reset demo data (sidebar). This puts the sample rate ($208.00 for 24k) and the sample stock back.

**On the phone**

1. Open the site. Watch the ticker: 24k, 22k and 18k per gram, and when the rate was set.
2. Tap **Price a piece**. Pick 22k, add a little weight, choose a making charge. See gold value, making charge, HST and the estimate.
3. Scroll to **Old gold**. Pick 22k and 10 g. The estimate is $1,792.33 at the sample buy rate. Tap **Book a valuation** and send it. (It lands in the admin under Reservations, Valuations.)
4. Open **Shop**. Tap **Large Light-Weight Tops**. See the full breakdown. Tap **Reserve this piece**.
5. In the bag choose a visit day (Monday is never offered) and a time window. Enter a name and a 10 digit mobile. Tap **Reserve my pieces**. You get a code like GM-4821.

**On the laptop**

6. Open **Reservations**. The new reservation is there within a couple of seconds, no refresh. The dashboard count went up and, in **Inventory**, the piece shows its units on hold.
7. Open **Gold rate**. Raise 24k by a dollar or two. Watch 22k, 18k and 14k move. Tap **Publish to website**.
8. Look at the phone. The ticker and every price on the shop have moved. The reservation you made keeps the rate it was made at.
9. On the laptop open the reservation and tap **Ready, text customer**. The phone's confirmation turns to Ready for pickup. Then tap **Mark sold**. A row appears in the sales ledger.
10. Open **Old gold**. Enter 10 g of 22k. Notice the **Record purchase** button is off until you turn on **Photo ID checked and copied**. Do that, add an ID reference and record it.
11. Open **Layaway**. Tap **Record** on Harpreet's plan. The payment is in the books.
12. Open **Books**. See sales, bullion, HST collected, purchases, HST to remit and the metal ledger. Add a supplier bill with a photo of the invoice.
13. Tap **Full accountant pack** to download the zip, or any CSV.
14. Tap **Send November books to Sam**. The send history grows. (It is a placeholder accountant, nothing leaves the app.)

Press **Reset demo data** when you are done.

## If something looks wrong

- Prices did not move on the phone: wait two seconds, the other device polls about once a second.
- A time window is greyed out: it has passed or it is full (three visits per window).
- You see a Neon error banner: the database is unreachable. Reload. Demo mode needs the database.
