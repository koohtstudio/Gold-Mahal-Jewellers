## 2. What a gold jeweller needs (research summary, drives the design)
- Daily gold rate by karat, published to the site and applied to every price.
- Pricing by weight: net weight x rate + making charge (percent or flat) + stone value. Making charge shown separately so customers trust it.
- Per-piece tags with karat, gross and net weight, stone details and certificate reference.
- Old gold purchase and exchange with tested purity, buy rate, ID check and a record of grams in and out (metal ledger).
- Custom orders and repairs tracked by ticket, quote, deposit and promise date.
- Layaway or instalment plans, reservations with a locked rate, appointments.
- Bullion (bars and coins) sold at rate plus a small premium, with its own tax treatment.
- Accountant-ready exports: sales, purchases, old gold, metal movement, HST summary.
- Website trust features that matter in jewellery: transparent pricing, certificates, clear policies, live chat or call, visit booking, large clean photography.
